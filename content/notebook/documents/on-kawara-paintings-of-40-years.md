---
title: "On Kawara: Paintings of 40 Years"
date: 2023-02-09
---

Kawara was born in Kariya, Japan on December 24, 1932. After graduating from Kariya High School in 1951, Kawara moved to [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo). Kawara went to Mexico in 1959, where his father was the director of an engineering company. He stayed three years, painting, attending art school and exploring the country. From 1962 to 1964 he moved back and forth between New York and Paris.
