"A mouse was placed in a glass cubic enclosing with a carved in space large enough to fit only the contours of it's body and infront of him was a small grey wall, This was all the mouse was allowed to see. The mouse was an actor in an experiment it had no hands in the making, it's participation will also be of no debate, at least not with the mouse itself.


After months of resistance to its unjust fate it became clear that giving in to the machination presented by the masters its mind fail to reduce tastes far better than bitter resistance, the wall had become... for a lack of better wording.. not that bad. its borders, it's unnecessity every detail was simple enough to engage its neural gears into a state of slow but steady churning, a kind of churn that seemed detrimental to halt. A belly dancers rhythmic waist gyrations slow kind of churn. Everyday, the reflections of the wall left a subtle but impressionable corona/mark on the mouses mental, this mark became more vivid and each new day was on a quest to outdo it's predecessor.

The mouse was learning to know the wall by blood, it had become beautiful to it, from its exact shade of gray, to the spots where its color values varied even the places in the wall where there where slight normally indistiguishable obtrusions on the walls surface.

This mouse savoring on the concoct of this most banal of vistas paired with a wetware circuited for learning, had the equivalent experience of an artist that painted a mural on a canvas with fading shades only to come back the next day to repaint the mural with slightly thicker values. But in this case the mural was the wall and the canvas and mouses being were intertwined as a single concept.

An eternity passed and the masters became bored. Seeking novelty they agreed to end their subjugation. The mouse was to be released from it's lock room. The glass was carved off it's skin. The grey wall removed and its liberty was once more jolsted upon him like a leash on a dog.

It's posture was fixed, identical to the enclosing it had years to break into and resisted the will to locomote. It remained at its sanctuary, the only place it knew and kept vigil as was written by Its perpetrators, it stared and studied and stared. Its Masters being worried decided to check in the woodened mouse for medical diagnostics. Their findings concluded that all was right with mouse with an exception of its ophthalmologic faculties. It was blind.

On and on in the loop of its mind the grey film, kept its static motion. With every frame indistiguishable from the next the pitiful animal drenched in elation for what new lie it brings for feed."

Actual Dream i had, wrote it down thinking of H.P Lovecraft.
